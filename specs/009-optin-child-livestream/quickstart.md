# Quickstart: Opt-In Child Livestream Validation

## Prerequisites
- Parent-child consent records configured
- Multi-camera zone map calibrated

## Scenario 1: Authorized child-only stream
1. Start session as opted-in parent.
2. Verify child-focused stream appears.
3. Verify other children are blurred.

## Scenario 2: Consent revocation during session
1. Start active session.
2. Revoke consent.
3. Verify immediate stream termination.

## Scenario 3: Camera handoff continuity
1. Move child between camera zones.
2. Verify smooth handoff with blur preserved.
